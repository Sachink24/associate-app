01 Privacy Policy

Solitaire Finz Mart ("we", "us", "our") operates as a Direct Selling Agent (DSA) and loan advisory business in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra, facilitating loan and financial products on behalf of partner banks and NBFCs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal and financial information submitted through our website, lead management platform, and related tools.

Information we collect may include: name, contact details, address, PAN/Aadhaar details (where required for KYC), income and employment information, property details, loan requirement details, CIBIL score and credit history references, and documents uploaded for underwriting purposes.

How we use it: to assess loan eligibility, match applicants with suitable lenders (banks/NBFCs), process applications, communicate status updates, and comply with regulatory requirements. We do not sell personal data to third parties. Data is shared only with the specific bank/NBFC selected for an application, and only to the extent required to process that application.

02 Terms & Conditions

By using this website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to the following terms:

  • Solitaire Finz Mart acts as an intermediary/DSA and does not itself sanction or disburse loans. Final approval, interest rates, and terms are determined solely by the partner bank/NBFC.
  • Submission of an enquiry or application does not guarantee loan approval.
  • You confirm that all information and documents provided are true, accurate, and belong to you or an applicant you are authorised to represent.
  • Processing fees, if any, are charged by the respective bank/NBFC as per their policy and are separately communicated; Solitaire Finz Mart's advisory role does not itself levy hidden charges.
  • We reserve the right to decline to process an enquiry that appears fraudulent, incomplete, or non-compliant with applicable regulations.

03 Data Storage & Processing

Lead, application, and workflow data submitted through our platform is stored on Supabase, a cloud database infrastructure provider, using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between your device and our servers. Access to stored data is restricted by role-based access control (RBAC) — only authorised Solitaire Finz Mart staff assigned to a given case can view its details.

We retain application data for as long as reasonably necessary to service the loan relationship and to meet statutory/regulatory record-keeping requirements applicable to DSA and financial advisory operations in India. Data may be deleted or anonymised upon request, subject to any legal retention obligations.

We do not use your data for unrelated marketing without consent, and we do not share credentials or documents outside the specific lender workflow for which they were submitted.

04 Your Rights

  • You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate information.
  • You may request deletion of your data, subject to statutory retention requirements tied to loan/KYC records.
  • You may withdraw consent for further contact at any time (this does not affect an already-submitted application under active processing with a lender).
